The question Which period products do people use, and does that change with age?

Pads and tampons still lead. But choices shift with age, and most people use more than one.

The products people can use shape their health, their ability to go to school and work, and how they feel. Researchers asked 7,394 study participants which period products they typically use. Everything below is what participants reported.

The takeaway

There is no one-size-fits-all period product.

The researchers found that most participants use more than one product. They say this makes sense because periods vary from day to day, from cycle to cycle and across a lifetime, and that people benefit from having a range of options to choose from.
